About Me

I am a data-driven professional with experience spanning Data Science, Analytics Engineering, and Data Platform Design, specializing in building robust, scalable, and insight-ready data solutions.

  • Modern Data Stack Expertise: dbt, BigQuery, Snowflake, Fivetran, Stitch, SQL, GCP
  • Orchestration & Engineering: Airflow, Python
  • Visualization & BI: Looker, Metabase, Tableau
  • Modeling Approaches: Star Schema, Data Vault, Data Warehouse Architecture

I have:

  • Architected enterprise-grade data warehouses to centralize and standardize analytics.
  • Automated ELT pipelines with Fivetran/Stitch + dbt, orchestrated via Airflow.
  • Built observable data pipelines with monitoring and alerting to ensure reliability and trust.
  • Implemented CI/CD workflows with GitHub to streamline testing, deployment, version control and collaboration.
  • Developed scalable data models and transformations in dbt and SQL, improving performance and reducing cost.
  • Enabled self-service analytics, empowering business teams to make data-driven decisions faster.

What sets me apart is the ability to bridge engineering and business needs — ensuring that technical solutions directly support strategy, growth, and efficiency.

Currently, I am focused on performance optimization, cost-efficient cloud architecture, and enabling teams to scale analytics capabilities across organizations.
